Players transform a large tropical island into a profitable resort while exploring, gathering resources, trading, completing quests, and dealing with alternatives and other island residents.

## 📖 Detailed Feature Guide

### Movement & Exploration

Resort Island Simulator uses first-person movement across the island and surrounding areas. Explore newly unlocked locations on foot and use available boats to travel across the water. Unlimited stamina is useful when covering long distances, gathering materials, farming, fishing, or moving between resort facilities.

### Tools & Inventory

The inventory stores gathered materials, food, fishing catches, crafted goods, and other items used for survival, production, construction, and trade. Unlimited resources can help maintain selected materials while building or producing, but avoid changing inventory values during transactions, crafting screens, or save operations.

### World & Environment

The game combines resort management with open-ended island activities. Gather wood and food, hunt, farm, fish, produce goods, unlock new areas, decorate the resort, construct service buildings, and trade with the outside world. Guests evaluate the resort through comfort and available services, while quests, events, alternatives, and other visitors create additional challenges.

### Combat & Weapons

Combat is part of the island's dangers, particularly during encounters involving alternatives and other hostile situations. Unlimited health provides defensive protection, while One-Hit Combat shortens fights. Activate combat options only after the player character has entered the game world, and disable them before saving or switching scenes.
